# About this data

**Source:** NBN Atlas occurrence download, accessed on 13 October 2026. Records provided by the British Trust for Ornithology (BTO), accessed through the NBN Atlas website, under the Open Government Licence.

- `sightings.csv`: bird records for the calendar years 2000, 2010 and 2020, from the "Birds (BTO/JNCC/RSPB partnership)" dataset. Mostly BTO Garden BirdWatch and BirdTrack, with smaller contributions from other surveys.
- One row per record: the date, the species, its family, the country, and the 10km grid square (with that square's latitude and longitude). Observer names, counts and other details aren't included in the published data. Locations of some sensitive species are blurred further.

The data provider's guidance: these records are good for exploring where and when species were recorded. They are not bird counts, not a pure Garden BirdWatch sample, and not directly comparable population estimates across years without accounting for survey effort and the mix of sources.
